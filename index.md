# YABA

A simple, private budgeting app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email [greenpocketprod@gmail.com](mailto:greenpocketprod@gmail.com) and include your device and the app version (Settings → About).

## Your data

Everything you enter stays on your device. If you turn on sync, YABA keeps a copy in a folder you choose in your own iCloud Drive. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.html).
