# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

YABA does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not connect to any server run by the developer.

## Where your data lives

- **On your device.** Your budgets, accounts and transactions are saved in a database inside the app on your device.
- **In your iCloud Drive, if you choose.** If you turn on sync, YABA saves copies of your budget to a folder you pick in your own iCloud Drive so your other devices can read them. Apple stores these files under your Apple ID; the developer cannot see them.
- **In files you export.** Backups and CSV exports are saved wherever you choose. What happens to them after that is up to you.

## Files you import

When you import a CSV or a backup, YABA reads it on your device only. Nothing is uploaded.

## Sharing

YABA never sells or shares your data with anyone.

## Children

YABA is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
